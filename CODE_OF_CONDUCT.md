# Community conduct

Harness³ welcomes participation across backgrounds and experience levels. These expectations apply to project issues, pull requests, discussions, and other project-managed spaces.

- Discuss code, evidence, and design decisions respectfully. Give actionable feedback and allow people to correct mistakes.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, publish personal information, or direct sexual attention at other participants.
- Keep reports and examples free of other people's credentials and private conversations.
- Disclose relevant conflicts of interest and avoid impersonation or misleading claims of project endorsement.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, close disruptive threads, or restrict participation. Responses should consider severity, context, and repeated behavior.

To report a conduct concern, open an issue with only a request for a private contact channel; omit personal details until a private channel is arranged. If the report concerns a maintainer, request a different maintainer where available. Use GitHub's platform reporting tools for abuse that requires independent review. Security vulnerabilities follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
